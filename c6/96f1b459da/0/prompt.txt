1. CFM runs a Start‑of‑Day (SOD) reconciliation once per US business day, early morning US Eastern, which:
Reconciles CFM balances to the clearinghouse reports (XTP/ION).  
Recalculates and posts intraday realized PnL and intraday fees into the CFM cash ledger, then resets those intraday stats.

So yes: there is a once‑per‑day batch that effectively moves daily_realized_pnl + fee adjustments into CFM USD cash, which then flows into your portfolio total USD.

2. The exact trigger time for the daily settlement is 4:30 AM ET (08:30 UTC)

Components rolled into the settlement include:
Realized P&L: Intraday profits or losses.
Fees: Trading fees incurred during the day.
Funding Costs: Any margin funding or fees-in-lieu-of-settle.
Ledger Adjustments: Reconciliation of unsettled balances.

3. The timing is Coinbase-specific and aligns with operational clearing runs rather than being a CFTC-required variation margin settlement. It ensures ledger reconciliation and margin adjustments are completed before the next trading day begins.

4. Yes, there is a statement endpoint that shows settlement postings as individual line items. You can access daily statements via:
https://www.coinbase.com/advanced-portfolio?tab=transactions

https://accounts.coinbase.com/statements/futures

5. The cost-basis method used for intraday realized P&L is FIFO (First-In-First-Out). However, discrepancies may arise due to fees, funding costs, or timing differences in settlement calculations.
If your reconstruction doesn't match the settlement amounts, it could be due to:

Fees: Intraday fees are not ledgered until the SOD process.
Unsettled P&L: Intraday realized P&L is reconciled during the daily settlement run.
